## What this site does

GridCraft.me helps people understand and use the GridCraft spreadsheet application. It brings together download links to official releases, installation advice, Excel compatibility notes, and practical workbook tutorials. The [guide library](/guides) covers everyday tasks such as importing data, checking formulas, and creating summaries. The [homepage](/) provides an overview and links to downloads.

The site also provides a browser entry point to the upstream spreadsheet application. Advice on this site and the software itself have separate maintainers. A problem with an article or a link belongs here; a reproducible problem in the application may also need an upstream bug report.

## Who maintains it

This site is maintained by an independent developer. No affiliation, professional credential, company identity, or team size is implied. You can reach the site maintainer at [support@gridcraft.me](mailto:support@gridcraft.me) or read the [contact page](/contact) for reporting guidance.

## How the content is prepared

Articles draw on the project's official documentation, tagged release notes, and small reproducible workbook examples. Sources are linked where they support a feature or compatibility claim. Hands-on reports identify the tested version and distinguish command-line calculations from interactions observed in the browser. A successful sample does not establish compatibility with every Excel workbook.

Screenshots illustrate the workflow; their captions identify upstream examples where applicable. Dates and version references describe the article's review context, rather than a promise that every screenshot reflects the latest interface. When a behavior has not been tested, the article should say so instead of presenting an assumption as a result.

Corrections are welcome. Please include the article URL, application version, and a small example that demonstrates the issue. Avoid sending confidential workbook data. Content can be revised when a report can be reproduced or an official source changes.
