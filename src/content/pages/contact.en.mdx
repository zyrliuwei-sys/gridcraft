## Email the site maintainer

Contact [support@gridcraft.me](mailto:support@gridcraft.me) for questions about this website, article corrections, broken download links, or privacy requests. This is the contact address for GridCraft.me, an independent guide, rather than the official GridCraft development team.

## Response time

This site is maintained by an independent developer. The target response time is 2–3 business days. This is a target rather than a guarantee; complex requests or periods of limited availability may take longer. If your question needs investigation, include enough detail for the maintainer to reproduce the issue without needing access to your private files.

## What to include

- The URL of the affected page and a short description of what happened.
- For an article correction, the relevant passage and a source or small reproducible example.
- For an application issue, the GridCraft version, operating system, browser if applicable, and the steps that led to the result.
- The result you expected and the result you actually saw.

Do not send passwords, account tokens, payment details, or confidential workbooks. A short sample with invented values is usually more useful than a complete working document. For a privacy request, explain the request in your message without adding unnecessary personal information.

## Website support and application support

The maintainer can investigate this website's content and links. GridCraft application development is managed by its own project; confirmed software bugs can be reported through the [official issue tracker](https://github.com/storytold/gridcraft/issues). Please check existing reports and remove private information before posting publicly.

For more context, read [About GridCraft.me](/about), our [Privacy Policy](/privacy-policy), or return to the [guide library](/guides).
