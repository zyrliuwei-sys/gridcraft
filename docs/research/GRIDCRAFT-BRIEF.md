## Design foundation

Reading this as a spreadsheet tool and learning hub for English-speaking Excel users, in Flora's spacious dark canvas language. Design variance 7, motion 3, density 4. Dark theme throughout. Body DM Sans; display Instrument Serif, chosen to reflect Flora's editorial serif and italic accents. Ink oklch(0.14 0.004 260), paper oklch(0.96 0.003 100), muted oklch(0.68 0.008 260), mint oklch(0.84 0.12 155), border oklch(0.29 0.008 260). Signature: floating interactive worksheet, chart and formula cards surrounding a central serif headline on a subtle dotted canvas.

## Reference topology and behavior

Flora: 72px dark sticky nav, centered serif hero at 52px at observed viewport, floating image canvas; body Geist, heading Marketing Redaction 10; background rgb(0,0,0), headline rgb(238,238,238). Hero followed by workflow/video sections, model overview, resource cards and CTA/footer. Desktop nav collapses to menu at mobile. The first workflow uses scroll progression; nav dropdowns use hover/click. Our adapted content uses real navigation, editable sample cells, resource filtering, download OS tabs, FAQ disclosures. The user asked for stylistic inspiration, not copied Flora assets or unrelated AI product content.
